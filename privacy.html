GEORGIA — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026 • App package: com.piraligame.georgia

GEORGIA is a single-player game developed by the GEORGIA / piraligame project. This policy describes how the Android app and its playable web version handle data.

Data we process

The game does not require user registration, collect names or contact information, or upload game progress to a developer server. Game progress, high scores, and language/audio preferences are stored locally on your device using browser/WebView storage. These data are used only to resume and personalize gameplay and can be deleted by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

Advertising, analytics, and payments

This version of GEORGIA does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, in-app payments, or accounts. The game does not sell or share player data with advertisers.

External websites

The game includes an optional link to the Georgian Gamers Guild Facebook page. Clicking it may open Facebook or a browser, which operates under that provider's separate privacy policy. The game's public website and GitHub source may be hosted by third parties, subject to their own infrastructure practices. The core packaged Android gameplay does not require an online account or an internet connection.

Children and data security

The game does not knowingly request or collect personal information from children. Local saved data are protected by the device's normal application-storage mechanisms; uninstalling or clearing app data removes saved progress.

Policy updates and contact

When the game's data practices change, this page will be updated. Contact the developer at piraligameofficial@gmail.com.
